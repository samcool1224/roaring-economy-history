BACKGROUND MUSIC (optional)

The museum already plays an original 1920s-style piano tune when a visitor
turns on "Music" (top bar or title screen). Nothing needs to go here.

To use your own recording instead, upload it to this folder named exactly:

    background.mp3

(.m4a, .ogg and .wav also work.) It loops quietly, gets quieter while
narration plays and pauses while an archival film is open. Pick music you
have the rights to use, such as a public-domain 1920s recording.
